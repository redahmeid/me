I've written a piece for the Equal Experts blog on why the line between engineers and architects has all but disappeared. When infrastructure was expensive and slow to change, an architect acting as gatekeeper made some sense. With elastic cloud and frequent deployments, that gatekeeping has become a bottleneck that takes ownership away from teams. Senior engineers and architects now share largely the same technical skills. What separates them is focus and time horizon: engineers concentrate on what needs delivering now, while architects think about how the same system will need to scale and evolve. The piece also looks at what AI-assisted engineering means for architects, and why influence, communication and empathy matter more than ever. It picks up a thread I started in 2021 with [Technology Pilots](/blog/technology-pilots).
